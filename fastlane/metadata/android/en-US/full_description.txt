Ink Keyboard is a keyboard for phones and readers with e-ink screens. It is based on HeliBoard and the AOSP keyboard and keeps their typing engine, dictionaries and layouts, but draws everything the way an e-ink panel likes it.

<b>Made for e-ink</b>
<ul>
<li>Keys are drawn as an outline and a label only: no fills, shadows or gradients</li>
<li>Pure black and white: text, icons and borders are rendered without antialiasing, so fast refresh modes stay crisp</li>
<li>Black on white or white on black</li>
<li>Adjustable border width, corner radius and label weight</li>
<li>No key preview popups and no animations; long-press popups, emoji, clipboard and suggestions appear instantly</li>
<li>Vibration confirms key presses; filling the pressed key is optional</li>
<li>Black and white emoji on the emoji panel</li>
<li>Hisense A9: optional full screen refresh after the keyboard closes (needs A9 Accessibility Service with broadcast control enabled)</li>
</ul>

<b>Private</b>
<ul>
<li>No internet permission: nothing you type leaves the device</li>
<li>Open source under GPL-3.0</li>
</ul>

<b>From HeliBoard</b>
<ul>
<li>Dictionaries for 16 languages included, with an extended Russian one; more can be added from a file</li>
<li>Many layouts, a number row, clipboard history, toolbar, one-handed mode</li>
</ul>
